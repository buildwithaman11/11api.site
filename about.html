About 11API

A free tool for generating AI voiceovers and lining them up with video — built to remove friction, not add an account you don't need.

What 11API does

11API lets you generate natural-sounding voiceovers using ElevenLabs or Gemini, then line each line up against a video's timeline by dragging the start and end points to exactly where they belong. It also works standalone — write and generate voice lines without uploading a video at all, and download the audio when you're done.

Why it's built the way it is

There's no sign-up, no account, and no server sitting in the middle. You bring your own ElevenLabs or Gemini API key, it's stored only in your browser's local storage, and every request goes directly from your browser to that provider's API. Your video file never leaves your device. This was a deliberate choice — the tool exists to do one job well, not to become another platform you have to manage a login for.

Who built this

11API is a solo project, built and maintained by Aman Babu Singh (buildwithaman11) — currently a first-year BCA student building tools like this in spare time between coursework. It started as a way to solve a specific annoyance (juggling separate TTS tabs while editing video) and grew from there.
